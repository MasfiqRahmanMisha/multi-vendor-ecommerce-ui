# 🛍️ Multi-Vendor E-Commerce Marketplace

### A Modern Marketplace for Customers, Vendors & Administrators

**Shop • Sell • Manage • Analyze**

[ HTML5 ] [ CSS3 ] [ JAVASCRIPT ] [ RESPONSIVE ] [ E-COMMERCE ]

---

Multi-Vendor E-Commerce Marketplace is a modern web-based
shopping platform designed to bring customers, vendors, and
administrators together in a single marketplace ecosystem.

---

## ✨ Overview

Multi-Vendor E-Commerce Marketplace is more than a simple
online store.

It is designed as a complete marketplace interface where
customers can discover and purchase products, vendors can
manage their stores, and administrators can oversee the
entire platform.

🎯 Core Idea: Create a realistic, modern and user-friendly
multi-vendor shopping experience.

---

---

## 🧑‍💻 User Roles

### 👤 Customer

Customers can browse products, explore categories,
view product details, manage their cart and continue
through the checkout experience.

### 🏪 Vendor

Vendors have their own workspace to manage products,
orders, sales information and earnings.

### ⚙️ Administrator

Administrators can monitor and manage the overall
marketplace through a dedicated dashboard.
